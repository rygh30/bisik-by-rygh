================================================================================
   INSTALLATION GUIDE — bisik by rygh (Adobe Premiere Pro Extension)
   Version: 1.1.0 | Platform: macOS Intel Edition (x86_64 Core i5/i7/i9/Xeon)
================================================================================

Thank you for choosing bisik by rygh!
This package installs 14 kinetic & documentary caption styles, procedural sound
effects, and offline AI speech transcription directly into Adobe Premiere Pro
for Intel-based Macs.

This standalone edition requires ZERO Homebrew setup and ZERO administrator
root commands. All processing components run portably in your user folder.

To complete installation on your Intel Mac, follow the 3 steps below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALL THE PREMIERE PRO EXTENSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click (or Control + click) on:
   "1_Install_Bisik_Extension.pkg"
2. Select "Open" from the context menu, then click "Open" on the security dialog.
   (macOS Sequoia 15+ note: If prompted by Gatekeeper, navigate to 
    System Settings > Privacy & Security > Security, and click "Open Anyway").
3. Follow the on-screen installer wizard:
   - Review and accept the End User License Agreement (EULA).
   - Enter your Mac password or Touch ID when requested.
4. Click "Close" when installation succeeds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: CONFIGURE THE AI & AUDIO ENGINE (FFmpeg & Whisper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click (or Control + click) on:
   "2_Setup_AI_Engine.command" and select "Open".
2. A Terminal window will launch automatically:
   - Whisper CLI is prebundled and copied directly to ~/whisper-cpp.
   - If FFmpeg is not already installed on your Mac, the script downloads 
     the portable static FFmpeg binary automatically.
   - When prompted for the speech model, Option [5] (Large-v3, ~3.1 GB) or 
     Option [6] (Large-v3-Turbo, ~1.6 GB) is recommended. For lighter setups, 
     Option [2] (Base, ~145 MB) or Option [3] (Small, ~460 MB) can be chosen.
3. Once the message "AI ENGINE SETUP COMPLETED SUCCESSFULLY" appears, 
   press any key to close the Terminal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: LAUNCH IN ADOBE PREMIERE PRO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Launch Adobe Premiere Pro.
2. In the top application menu bar, navigate to:
   Window > Extensions > bisik by rygh
3. The panel is now ready to transcribe audio and generate kinetic captions!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING & SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you experience any issues, refer to "TROUBLESHOOTING.txt" included in this

================================================================================
PRIVACY & ARCHITECTURE NOTICE
================================================================================
- 100% Local & Offline: All audio extraction (FFmpeg) and artificial 
  intelligence transcription (Whisper.cpp) execute entirely on your machine.
  No audio, media clips, or transcripts are ever transmitted to external servers.
- System Requirements:
  - macOS 10.15 (Catalina), 11.0 (Big Sur), 12.0 (Monterey), 13.0 (Ventura),
    14.0 (Sonoma), or 15.0 (Sequoia) on Intel Core or Xeon processors.
  - Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (v22.0) through 2025 (v25.x) or later.
  - Recommended minimum 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended for Large-v3 model).
