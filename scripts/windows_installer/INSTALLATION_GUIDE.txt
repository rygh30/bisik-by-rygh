================================================================================
   INSTALLATION GUIDE - bisik by rygh (Adobe Premiere Pro Extension)
   Version: 1.1.0 | Platform: Windows 10 & Windows 11 (64-bit)
================================================================================

Thank you for choosing bisik by rygh!
This package installs 14 kinetic and documentary caption styles, procedural sound
effects, and offline AI speech transcription directly into Adobe Premiere Pro.

To complete installation on Windows, follow the 3 steps below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALL THE PREMIERE PRO EXTENSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Choose ONE of the two methods below to install the extension:

METHOD A (Fastest - 1-Click Batch Installer):
  1. Double-click "1_Install_Extension.bat".
     (If prompted by Windows SmartScreen, click "More info" -> "Run anyway").
  2. The script will automatically unpack the extension into your Adobe CEP
     directory and configure the required PlayerDebugMode registry keys.
  3. Press any key to finish when prompted.

METHOD B (Standard Adobe ZXP Installer):
  1. Download and open the free ZXP Installer tool: https://zxpinstaller.com/
  2. Drag and drop "bisik_by_rygh.zxp" onto the ZXP Installer window.
  3. Follow the prompts until the extension installation is confirmed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: CONFIGURE THE AI & AUDIO ENGINE (FFmpeg & Whisper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "2_Setup_AI_Engine.bat".
2. A Command Prompt window will open:
   - The script will automatically verify, download, and configure portable
     binaries for FFmpeg and Whisper CLI directly into your user profile.
   - When prompted for the speech recognition model, Option [5] (Large-v3, ~3.1 GB)
     is strongly recommended for maximum accuracy and punctuation precision.
     For lighter systems, Option [2] (Base, ~145 MB) or Option [3] (Small, ~460 MB)
     can be chosen.
3. Once the message "AI ENGINE SETUP COMPLETED" appears, press any key to close.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: LAUNCH IN ADOBE PREMIERE PRO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Launch Adobe Premiere Pro.
2. In the top application menu bar, navigate to:
   Window > Extensions > bisik by rygh
3. The panel is now ready to transcribe audio and generate kinetic captions!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING & SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you experience any issues, refer to "TROUBLESHOOTING.txt" included in this
package for immediate solutions to common questions.

================================================================================
PRIVACY & ARCHITECTURE NOTICE
================================================================================
- 100% Local & Offline: All audio extraction (FFmpeg) and artificial 
  intelligence transcription (Whisper.cpp) execute entirely on your machine.
  No audio, media clips, or transcripts are ever transmitted to external servers.
- System Requirements:
  - Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) or Windows 11 (64-bit).
  - Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 (v22.0) through 2025 (v25.x) or later.
  - Recommended minimum 8 GB RAM / system memory.
